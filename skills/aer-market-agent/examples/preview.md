# What this skill produces

Running `python scripts/build_report.py` generates an 18-slide PowerPoint:

1. Title
2. What the DMO is - the basics
3. How the AER builds a DMO bill (calculation + worked example)
4. The seven-year price trajectory (all zones)
5. Where your dollar goes - cost-stack doughnut
6. Every component, tracked year by year
7. Adjust for inflation - nominal vs real
8. In real terms, the network peaked a decade ago
9. Network costs, decomposed (building blocks)
10. Every DMO-region network just reset
11. Rising cost, flat grid (average + peak demand)
12. Why the network bill rose - not demand
13. Same year, opposite directions (regional divergence)
14. Falling usage is reshaping your bill (tariff shift)
15. DMO 8: the rules changed (2026 reforms)
16. **Your analysis** - the fork point for your own slide
17. Close - branding + open-source call to action
18. Method & sources (with clickable references)

Driven by `data/dmo_dataset.json`; see `DATA_SOURCES.md` to refresh, and the
"Verification discipline" section in `SKILL.md` before extending it.
